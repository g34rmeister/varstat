## Acknowledgement
VarStat's menus, model language and methods follow **Sisvar**, the statistics program by
Prof. Daniel Furtado Ferreira (Departamento de Estatística, Universidade Federal de Lavras, Brazil).
VarStat is an independent program written from the published statistical methods. It contains
no Sisvar code, help text or data, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by its author or UFLA.
Sisvar: https://des.ufla.br/~danielff/sisvar_windows11.html

If VarStat's methods support your work, the citations Sisvar's author recommends are:
- Ferreira, D. F. (2019). Sisvar: a computer analysis system to fixed effects split plot type designs. *Revista Brasileira de Biometria*, 37(4), 529–535.
- Ferreira, D. F. (2014). Sisvar: a guide for its bootstrap procedures in multiple comparisons. *Ciência e Agrotecnologia*, 38(2), 109–112.
- Ferreira, D. F. (2011). Sisvar: a computer statistical analysis system. *Ciência e Agrotecnologia*, 35(6), 1039–1042.
