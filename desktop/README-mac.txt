VarStat for macOS
=================
Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4): VarStat-mac-apple-silicon.zip
Intel Macs:                  VarStat-mac-intel.zip

1. Double-click the zip. Drag VarStat.app to your Applications folder.
2. First launch: Control-click (right-click) VarStat.app and choose Open, then Open again.
   (VarStat is not from the App Store, so macOS asks once.)
   On macOS 15 or later: if there is no Open button, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security,
   scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to VarStat.
   Terminal alternative:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VarStat.app
3. You can also drag CSV, Excel (.xlsx), ODS or Sisvar .fdt files onto the VarStat icon.

Language: choose English, Português, Español or Tagalog in the top-right corner of the window.

PT  Clique com o botão direito em VarStat.app > Abrir na primeira vez (ou Ajustes do Sistema > Privacidade e Segurança > Abrir Mesmo Assim).
ES  La primera vez, haga clic derecho en VarStat.app > Abrir (o Ajustes del Sistema > Privacidad y seguridad > Abrir igualmente).
TL  Sa unang bukas, i-right-click ang VarStat.app > Open (o System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway).
